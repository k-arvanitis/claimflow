# Policy corpus sources

ClaimFlow's local retrieval corpus contains:

- `cms_medicare_claims_processing_manual_ch26.pdf` — official CMS Medicare
  Claims Processing Manual, Chapter 26, “Completing and Processing Form
  CMS-1500 Data Set”:
  https://www.cms.gov/Regulations-and-Guidance/Guidance/Manuals/downloads/clm104c26.pdf
- `cms_npi_fact_sheet.pdf` — official CMS “The National Provider Identifier”
  fact sheet:
  https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/administrative-simplification/nationalprovidentstand/downloads/npi_fs_geninfo_010906.pdf
- `health_policy.pdf`, `property_policy.pdf`, and `loan_policy.pdf` — synthetic
  demonstration policies generated by `scripts/generate_policies.py`. These are
  useful for exercising retrieval but are not insurer contracts or authoritative
  coverage policies.

The CMS documents support form-completion and identifier requirements. They do
not replace a payer's plan document, Medicare Administrative Contractor local
coverage determination, or current licensed CPT content.
